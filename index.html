The Residence
Offered for the first time in over a decade

A craftsman residence on a quiet corner beside Heritage Park, moments from downtown Kirkland, with sweeping views of Lake Washington, the Olympics, and Mt. Rainier.

Built in 2002 in the Pacific Northwest craftsman tradition, Waverly Nest unfolds across three dramatic levels and 3,580 square feet, with four bedrooms, three and a half baths, and a private elevator serving every floor. A showstopping glass staircase and bridge, crafted by Seattle Glassworks, anchors the entry and earned a 2004 BALA Platinum award for Best Detail in a Home: the architectural centerpiece of a residence built with uncommon craft throughout.

The home sits just off Heritage Park, on a quieter corner with a sweeping view that takes in Lake Washington, the Bellevue and Seattle skylines, the Olympic Mountains, and Mt. Rainier: an exposure that fills the upper floors with sunset light. Downtown Kirkland's waterfront, restaurants, and galleries are an easy walk away, as is Waverly Beach.

Addresses that pair this degree of walkability with unobstructed lake and skyline views rarely come to market in downtown Kirkland. Waverly Nest is offered for the first time in over a decade.

Design & Craftsmanship

A BALA Platinum award for Best Detail in a Home

In January of 2004, this home received a BALA Platinum award for Best Detail in a Home. Spanning the entry that rises through all three levels, a fan-shaped glass bridge crafted by Seattle Glassworks connects the upper floors while an open riser stair with cantilevered glass treads ties the levels together, alongside a private elevator serving each floor. This design makes a dramatic impact upon entering the home and showcases the inherent duality of glass, a material that is transparent yet solid: transmitting both mass and light. Well crafted, the glass bridge and stair blend well with the craftsman style of the home.

The Layout

Three levels, thoughtfully arranged

Main Floor

The primary suite anchors this level, with its own fireplace, private patio, two walk-in closets, and a sumptuous ensuite. Two additional bedrooms open directly onto the low-maintenance English garden.

Top Floor

Kitchen and living space combine into one airy, light-filled retreat. French doors open to a Juliette balcony over the garden, and a large patio with a retractable awning invites day-to-night living, stargazing, and west-facing views.

Third Level

A fourth bedroom with its own ensuite sits alongside a cozy bonus room, with access to the three-car garage and shop below.

Downtown Kirkland

Downtown Kirkland is one of the Eastside's most walkable neighborhoods, a compact grid of waterfront parks, independent restaurants, wine bars, and galleries, all within a few blocks of Lake Washington. Heritage Park, just steps from the home, offers open lawn and lake access away from the busier main waterfront, and Waverly Beach Park, with its own swimming dock, is close by as well.

Seattle is roughly twenty minutes away via the SR-520 floating bridge, and Bellevue's business district is closer still. Both are visible from the home's upper floors, part of a sweeping panorama that takes in Lake Washington, the Bellevue and Seattle skylines, the Olympic Mountains, and Mt. Rainier: a rare combination of lakeside, small-town character with mountains and two skylines in view, and easy access to either city.
